# The Anatomy of Pace

**Principal Investigator:** Dr. Anatomy Pace  
**A quantitative framework for physiological telemetry.**

---

### Mission

This laboratory is dedicated to the deconstruction of trail running. The objective is mathematical precision in analyzing pace degradation, terrain tax (TI), and biomechanical efficiency.

**Dr. Anatomy Pace** is the public principal investigator and owner of *The Anatomy of Pace* — including this repository, the research pipeline, donor deliverables (*Kinematic_Scan*), and all public channels (GitHub, Substack, Instagram).

### Scope boundary (non-negotiable)

*The Anatomy of Pace* is a **standalone research brand**. Private training periodization for internal subjects is **not** part of this project, must **never** be published under this name, and must **never** be linked from public Anatomy of Pace channels.

### Lab principles

* **Data-driven:** All conclusions are anchored in raw telemetry from `.fit` files.
* **Clinical aesthetic:** Visualizations are functional, not decorative.
* **Ghost Authority:** Analysis precedes personal narrative. The telemetry indicates; the algorithm quantifies.

### Toolbox

* **Engine:** Python 3.x
* **Core libraries:** `pandas`, `numpy`, `matplotlib` (F1-style telemetry visualization)
* **Workflow:** `.fit` → data cleaning → TI calculation → visualization
