### **Section 2: Ulvaskog-traversen (7–10 km)**

* **2A & 2B:** \+134m climb to naked slabs and dense woods. Pace drops to 14:26 \- 18:14 min/km.  
* **Micro Terrain:** Narrow singletrack dominated by slick mud, dense root networks, and highly uneven bare rock. High risk of slipping requires constant ankle stabilization.

### **Section 3: Gloppavatn-dalen (10–13 km)**

* **3A, 3B, & 3C:** Technical descent and raw forest terrain leading into the station.  
* **Micro Terrain:** Technical forest paths heavily loaded with loose stones and roots. The climb towards Preikestolhytta introduces steep "sherpatrapper" (stone steps) and dry "svaberg" (slickrock), causing severe eccentric loading on the quads.

### **Section 4: Fjellovergangen / Neverdalsskaret (13–17 km)**

* **4A to 4D:** Massive stone steps transitioning to raw DNT trail. Pace fluctuates heavily.  
* **Micro Terrain:** High-altitude mountain terrain consisting of bare rock, low heather, and soft, mossy ground without any protective planking.

### **Section 5: Mot Bakken Gård (17–22 km)**

* **Micro Terrain:** A notorious, extremely technical section consisting of massive boulders and large stone blocks right at the fjord's edge. Running is impossible; requires dynamic, full-body movement and high proprioceptive focus.
